   206→   206→## 5. Product mission and first sellable workflow
   207→   207→
   208→   208→RescueLoop is a student-success and recurring-revenue recovery product for Whop course creators.
   209→   209→
   210→   210→The first sellable end-to-end workflow is:
   211→   211→
   212→   212→1. A creator installs RescueLoop in Whop.
   213→   213→2. RescueLoop validates administrator access and required permissions.
   214→   214→3. The creator maps a Whop product/course.
   215→   215→4. RescueLoop performs a truthful first sync.
   216→   216→5. RescueLoop identifies paid members who have not started after a configurable threshold.
   217→   217→6. The creator opens the Rescue Queue.
   218→   218→7. The creator sees understandable evidence explaining why a member was flagged.
   219→   219→8. RescueLoop drafts a respectful message grounded only in known evidence.
   220→   220→9. The creator edits, approves, schedules, dismisses, or suppresses the intervention.
   221→   221→10. A final safety check runs immediately before provider submission.
   222→   222→11. The Whop provider accepts the notification request idempotently.
   223→   223→12. RescueLoop observes response or course return.
   224→   224→13. The outcome appears in a clear evidence timeline.
   225→   225→14. Value is attributed honestly as confirmed, strongly associated, or estimated.
   226→   226→15. The creator receives evidence-backed course-friction insights.
   227→   227→
   228→   228→Do not allow feature breadth to compromise this core loop.
